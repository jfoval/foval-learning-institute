# Reviews: Algebra Essentials — course-wide

Append-only. Newest at the bottom.

## Standing decisions for this course

- **The neutrality pass is skipped on every lesson**, per the Stage 4 rule and the outline's
  neutrality section. There are no contested or value claims in evaluating an expression or solving
  a linear equation. The one place a value question could enter, whether algebra should be required
  of everybody, is an education-policy argument the course does not raise. Auditing "solve for x"
  for ideological balance would be money spent on nothing.
- **The fact-check is executed, not fetched.** Every worked example, table row, exercise answer,
  quiz option and quiz explanation is computed by the reviewer rather than checked against a source.
  A wrong sign in a worked example teaches the error directly.
- **Two lessons a session is allowed**, as the pipeline permits for procedural courses.

## 2026-09-18 — CW: scope is a deliberate decision, and it stops short of two things

Raised alongside the Python note (CW-04 there) when John asked whether this course is too short.
Its bodies average 2,413 words against 5,800 for courses designed from scratch, the lowest in the
institute.

**Half of the gap is honest.** A worked example carries in twenty lines what exposition would need
two hundred for, and `research/OUTLINE.md` fixes the internal shape deliberately: question, worked
example, completion problem, misconception, interleaved problems. Word count is the wrong measure
of a procedural lesson and the outline says so.

**The other half is scope, and unlike Python's it was reasoned.** The outline argues the sequence
is forced (variable, equivalence, solving, lines, translating, systems) and adds a sixth lesson
rather than inheriting five. So the six that exist are the right six. What it never asks is whether
six is enough for what the Core path puts downstream of it: **exponents and roots**, which Geometry
and Trigonometry needs for Pythagoras, and **quadratics**, which anything with an area or a
trajectory needs. Neither appears.

**Not acted on.** The six lessons are at standard and are not re-opened. When this course comes up
in Core order (Term 5), it gains lessons; it does not lose any.
