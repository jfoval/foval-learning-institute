# Reviews: Reading Well lesson 1, "Reading is not one activity"

## 2026-09-17 — Lesson 01 — depth, fact-check, neutrality, pedagogy, voice and media, Tier A

**Reviewer:** one subagent, fresh context, all five passes. Tier A chosen because the lesson is not
in a standards 3.4 sensitive domain, is not a standpoint course, and had not been sent back.

**Verified clean before the findings, and worth recording so a later pass does not redo it:** both
Bacon quotations word for word including the gloss after the semicolon; all five Adler quotations
verbatim with no OCR artefacts; Brysbaert's 190 studies, 18,573 participants and 238 wpm; Carver's
five gear figures and labels; the `unread:` line respected, with zero hits for Seidenberg, Willingham
and Richards; quiz option spreads of 12, 14, 11, 2 and 14 characters with the key never the longest,
all four answer indices used and no adjacent repeat; no em dash, no spaced en dash, no banned
vocabulary or sentence shape; `minutes: 70` matching the measured figure.

**Findings:**

1. [major] **L01-F01, fact-check.** Both footnote markers read `[1]`, which is Bacon, while the
   claims they sit on are Carver's gears and Brysbaert's meta-analysis, which are `[3]`. The Bacon
   and Adler quotations carried no markers at all, against 2.1.
2. [major] **L01-F02, depth and fact-check.** "That is the finding this lesson is built on" named a
   finding that does not exist, and two invented frequencies, "perhaps a third of the time" and
   "perhaps a quarter of the time", were stated with the authority of data. Nothing in SOURCES.md
   measures how often a reader's level matches their purpose.
3. [major] **L01-F03, depth and fact-check.** The lesson's central claim, that effort is set by what
   you need to do afterwards, is the drafter's synthesis of Bacon, Adler and Carver and was presented
   with no attribution. Bacon does not support it: his gloss predicates the treatment of books, not
   of purpose. And "construction is where the time goes" is a claim about the cognitive mechanism of
   reading time, which SOURCES.md's own scope rule forbids without Willingham or Seidenberg read.
4. [major] **L01-F04, pedagogy.** Quiz 5 marked as "the commonest misreading" a view Bacon states
   plainly in the same essay ("the meaner sort of books") and which the lesson itself endorses eighty
   lines later. The key is Adler's position, not Bacon's.
5. [major] **L01-F05, media and licensing 4.5.** The body and Go deeper both linked the Internet
   Archive scan of Adler as "readable free". The item is a community upload with no licence and no
   rights statement, and the book is in copyright. See `course-wide.md`.
6. [major] **L01-F06, fact-check.** The heading "The same idea, arrived at by measurement" and the
   line "the gears are real" overstate Carver, whom Brysbaert says "ventured" the model and
   "estimated" its speeds, and whom Brysbaert is partly arguing against.
7. [moderate] **L01-F07, fact-check, defect 9.** The Carver caveat said his figures "were calibrated
   against" the 300 wpm norm, dropping Brysbaert's "but was also established independently". The cut
   ran in the direction that helped the lesson. SOURCES.md carried the same overstatement.
8. [moderate] **L01-F08, depth.** The four-ways example gives an inspection of a twelve-page article
   ten minutes; the checkpoint eighty lines earlier gives a reader forty minutes for the same output
   and then defends the choice.
9. [moderate] **L01-F09, pedagogy, defect 5b.** Quiz 4 restated a checkpoint's stem and its hidden
   answer; quiz 2 replayed the tenancy example verbatim.
10. [moderate] **L01-F10, fact-check.** "Analytical reading asks whether what the author says is
    true" is not Adler's question for the level. He calls it "preeminently for the sake of
    understanding" and puts judgement last, after eight of his fifteen rules.
11. [moderate] **L01-F11, depth.** "Scanned, which is elementary reading aimed at one target" imports
    Carver's fifth gear under Adler's first level, in a section presenting itself as the four levels
    and next to a diagram that shows them.
12. [moderate] **L01-F12, fact-check.** Adler's chapter 2 is five pages, not eight, and it is not
    "the source of everything above", which includes Bacon and Carver.
13. [moderate] **L01-F13, fact-check.** The tenancy checkpoint told the reader to look for the
    agreement's definition "since a contract usually defines its terms somewhere". "Fair wear and
    tear" is a term of art whose meaning comes from outside the document, so agreements routinely do
    not define it and its absence is the norm rather than a warning sign.
14. [moderate] **L01-F14, fact-check, defect 9.** Adler's inspectional aim was quoted as "to get the
    most out of a book within a given time", cutting his own concession that it is "always (by
    definition) too short a time to get out of the book everything that can be gotten".
15. [minor] **L01-F15, voice, defect 7.** One "Here is" section opener and nine paragraphs closing on
    a compressed one-line moral.
16. [minor] **L01-F16, fact-check.** The *Essays* were first published in 1597, enlarged in 1612 and
    1625; the lesson said "published his *Essays* in 1625".
17. [minor] **L01-F17, depth.** The body did not say Carver was read through Brysbaert, though
    `## Sources` did, against the outline's own convention.
18. [minor] **L01-F18, depth, defect 1.** Adler's own sentence about readers who "start a book on page
    one and plow steadily through it" sat unused in a source read in full, two lines from quotations
    the lesson already uses.
19. [minor] **L01-F19, depth.** The reading rates were given with no note that they are for adult
    native English readers, a limit SOURCES.md flags explicitly.
20. [minor] **L01-F20, pedagogy.** "Now do the next step yourself before reading on" is defect 4's
    tell phrase and was redundant beside the checkpoint that follows it.
21. [minor] **L01-F21, accessibility.** The SVG's "Inspectional" label used `--gold`, about 3.1:1 on
    the light ground, under AA for 15px text. The palette already carries `--gold-text` for this.
22. [minor] **L01-F22, opportunity.** Adler is himself one of the people who quotes the Bacon line
    and stops at the halfway point, and the lesson quotes him doing it without noticing.
23. [minor] **L01-F23, housekeeping.** `research/reviews/` did not exist.

**Neutrality:** not a 3.4 domain and not applicable beyond one value question. Adler's canon is
genuinely set aside in the body as SOURCES.md promised. The leak the reviewer found was **Bacon's**
value judgement rather than Adler's: the lesson quoted "the meaner sort of books" and endorsed the
ranking in its own voice, ninety lines after telling the reader that treatment depends on the reader's
need and not on the book alone. Presentism clean in both directions. Perspectives checked against: a
defender of Adler's canon-centred view of education, a reader who believes reading every word is a
moral obligation, the speed-reading industry, and second-language readers.

**Resolutions applied, 2026-09-17:**

   quotations.

   with the claim stated as the course's own bet about readers.

   "Construction is where the time goes" is gone, replaced by what the reader must be able to

4. F04 fixed. Quiz 5 re-stemmed to ask what Adler's use of the line adds that Bacon's own sentence
   does not say, and the explanation no longer calls the quality reading a misreading of Bacon.
5. F05 fixed in the lesson: both links removed from the body and Go deeper, replaced with Open
   Library and a note that it is in most libraries. The scan stays in `## Sources` as the text
   consulted. **Escalated to John**, because it also affects SOURCES.md's framing and the outline's
   plan for lesson 2. Recorded in `course-wide.md`.
6. F06 fixed. Heading is now "A psychologist got to the same place from a different direction", and

7. F07 fixed in the lesson and in `research/SOURCES.md`, which carried the same overstatement.
8. F08 fixed. The checkpoint's article is now a thirty-page report, so forty minutes is a defensible

9. F09 fixed. Quiz 4 re-cased onto an equipment manual and a new pair of readers; quiz 2 moved from

14. F14 fixed. The quotation runs to the end of Adler's sentence.
15. F15 fixed. The "Here is" opener is gone and six of the nine one-line closers absorbed into their

**Declined:** nothing. Every finding was acted on.

**Status after review:** needs second pass. The reviewer asked for one because four of the six major
findings sat in a single failure mode, the lesson claiming more evidential backing than it had, and
that is the mode this course can least afford. The second pass should check the rewritten sections
rather than the whole lesson.


## 2026-09-17 — Lesson 01 — second pass on the rewrites, Tier A

**Reviewer:** one subagent, fresh context, asked to check the 23 resolutions rather than re-review
the lesson, and to assume the fix pass introduced new defects. It did: three of its four substantive
findings were created by the first fix pass, which is the failure mode a second pass exists to catch.

**Findings:**

1. [major] **L01-F24.** F02 was only half done. "Perhaps a quarter of the time" survived byte for byte
   in the misconceptions list, while the review file and the commit message both said both invented
   fractions were cut. A false record is worse than the defect.
2. [major] **L01-F25.** The Carver fix stopped short of what Brysbaert concludes. His general
   discussion is headed "There is no evidence for reading gears except for the distinction between
   reading and scanning", and he reports failing to find a difference between reading for
   recognition and reading out of interest, no bimodal curve, and no difference in kind for reading
   for recall. The lesson had moved from "the gears are real" to "a proposal, not a result" and
   still told the reader to keep the five-way ordering, which is the one thing the source rejects.
   The refutation sits in a part of the paper `SOURCES.md` recorded as unread.
3. [moderate] **L01-F26.** Quiz 5's new explanation said Adler "would reject" the view that tasting
   is usually enough, and then asserted the same view two clauses later as "most books stop early by
   design". Adler in fact writes that analytical reading "is hardly ever necessary" for information
   or entertainment.
4. [moderate] **L01-F27.** Quiz 4's re-casing confounded purpose with prior knowledge: a technician
   and a buyer differ in background as much as in goal, the stem never said they were asked for
   different things, and the distractor naming prior knowledge was as good as the key.
5. [moderate] **L01-F28.** The F08 fix made the timing worse, not better. The checkpoint gave forty
   minutes for thirty pages against the lesson's own hour for twelve, a factor of four.
6. [moderate] **L01-F29.** The tenancy answer stated a legal fact with no source, in a course whose
   research file has nothing on tenancy law, and told the reader to look the phrase up without
   saying where.
7. [moderate] **L01-F30.** "Also arrived at independently from Carver's own work on compressed
   speech" reads as "independently of", inverting Brysbaert, who says the estimate came partly from
   that work. Footnote 3 repeated the error and attributed to "the norm" what Brysbaert says of
   Carver's own estimate.
8. [minor] **L01-F31 to F37.** Reviewer's word "predicates" lifted into learner prose; "estimated"
   in quotation marks though Brysbaert uses it once and of scanning only; an Adler quotation
   silently repunctuated to avoid his em dash; three new "worth" hedges and a reinstated "here is"
   opener; the opening quoting Adler before the reader has felt the problem; two more unmeasured
   frequency claims; and the four-ways section still crossing Adler's levels with Carver's scanning.
9. [minor] **L01-F38.** Bookkeeping: the review file said quiz 2 moved to an insurance policy when
   it moved to a grant application; `docs/QUEUE.md` was not updated in the same commit as the work,
   against root rule 4; `minutes` read 75 against a declared 70.

**Resolutions applied, 2026-09-17:**

1. F24 fixed, and the record corrected: resolution 2 above overstated what the first pass did.
2. F25 fixed properly, which meant reading Brysbaert's general discussion rather than hedging around
   it. The section is now "A psychologist tried to measure this, and half of it did not survive". It
   states the gears, states that the review rejects the middle distinctions, and keeps the two
   things that survive: reading against scanning is real, and reading for recall is slower because
   of rereading and restructuring rather than because it is another mode. **That second finding is
   the lesson's own claim arriving from the measurement side**, so the section is stronger than the
   version that misreported the source. A new `:::predict` carries it. `SOURCES.md` and
   `course-wide.md` corrected to match, and the Brysbaert read-depth label now says the general
   discussion was read.
3. F26 fixed. The explanation no longer claims Adler rejects it, and rules B out on the ground the
   stem actually tests.
4. F27 fixed. Both readers are now engineers on the same team, asked for different things in the
   stem, which licenses the key and rules out the prior-knowledge distractor.
5. F28 fixed. The checkpoint now says plainly that neither reader read thirty pages closely, and
   that the second surveyed first and chewed the two or three pages carrying the argument, which
   also foreshadows the tenancy section.
6. F29 fixed. The answer now names the tenancy deposit schemes as the body that would adjudicate,
   links their guidance, says the sense is fixed by the law where you rent, and says this is reading
   practice rather than legal advice.
7. F30 fixed in the body, in footnote 3 and in `SOURCES.md`.
8. F31 to F37 all fixed: "attaches" for "predicates"; the "estimated" quotation gone with the
   rewritten section; the Adler quotation split so his sentence is carried whole without
   repunctuating it; the "worth" hedges and the "here is" opener removed; the opening restored to
   scene first and Adler second, as confirmation; the remaining frequency claims reworded; and the
   four-ways section now explains scanning's presence by the one distinction that survived
   measurement, which turns the crossing from an inconsistency into the point.
9. F38 fixed. The insurance-policy line corrected above, the queue updated in this commit, and
   `minutes` set to the measured 80.

**Declined:** nothing.

**Status after review:** clean. Two rounds is where this stops unless a later reader finds
something; the second pass's own findings were concentrated in what the first fix pass changed, and
those sections have now been rewritten from the source rather than patched.
