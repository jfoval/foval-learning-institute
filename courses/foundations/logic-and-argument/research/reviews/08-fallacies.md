## Lesson 8, "Fallacies". Stage 4 round 1 + fixes, 2026-09-06

One reviewer, all five passes in this context, fixes applied in the same session. **Verdict: the
lesson clears the fallacy-lesson trap by a wide margin.** It is not a listicle: every fallacy
taught carries its mechanism, its cogent twin, and the critical questions that separate them; the
Hamblin/Finocchiaro/Massey/Boudry critique is taught at full strength alongside Walton and the
pragma-dialecticians; the twelve-passage practice mixes four cogent cases in with eight fallacious
ones so the learner practises the discrimination, not the labelling. It also arrived with 23 body
links, zero em dashes, zero banned vocabulary, a spread answer key (1,0,3,2,1,0), and six of six
quiz items genuine application. What failed: one sign error inherited from a bad PDF extraction,
two unread-source citations, one SVG breaking every rule at once, and the recurring
answer-leak and free-recall defects.
