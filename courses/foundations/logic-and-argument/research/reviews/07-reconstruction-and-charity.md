## Lesson 7, "Reconstruction and charity". Stage 4 round 1 + fixes, 2026-09-06

One reviewer, all five passes in this context, fixes applied in the same session. **Verdict: the
teaching design was already strong** (the boiler worked example is a genuine move-by-move

### Depth (Part 1)

- **L7-D1 [major, FIXED].** The principle of charity was introduced as "old and plain" with no
  history, in the lesson named for it. The idea has a documented lineage (Wilson 1959 coined the
  name; Quine used it for translation; Davidson made it a condition of interpretation; informal
  logic borrowed it for reconstruction). A paragraph added, sourced to SEP "Donald Davidson"
  sec. 3.3 (read), with the Wilson attribution honestly flagged as standard-but-secondhand in the
  source note. The paragraph earns its place: the uncharitable reconstruction fails the same way
  the uncharitable translation does.
- **L7-D2 [major, FIXED].** Hitchcock was cited as "stressing" that warrants are field-dependent.
  The paper argues nearly the opposite: field-dependency is Toulmin's thesis and Hitchcock
  qualifies it ("Many warrants belong to definite fields ... But many do not. Some are
  common-sense generalizations"). Corrected, and the qualification now does teaching work: the
  boiler's fifteen-year rule is exactly a fieldless common-sense warrant.
- **L7-D3 [major, FIXED].** Lesson 5 promises "Lesson 7 returns to conductive arguments when it
  separates linked from convergent support"; the word "conductive" appeared nowhere in the lesson.
  One sentence added in the linked/convergent section tying conductive arguments to pure
  convergent support.
- **L7-D4 [minor, FIXED].** Sources 7 (Copi) and 9 (Hurley) were listed but cited by no body
  marker: dead weight from unread books. Removed with the rest (see integrity).
- **L7-D5 [not fixed, reasoned].** The outline wanted worked example (b) built on "a real passage
  (from Van Cleave ch. 1's CC BY exercises, or a documented public text)". The lesson uses an
  invented boiler message, labelled as such. SOURCES.md records why: Van Cleave's 1.10 exercises
  are mostly political and unusable under the course's example rules, and no clean documented
  non-political passage was sourced at draft time. The boiler passage is realistic multi-sentence
  prose worked move by move (close analysis, standard form, gap, diagram, ARG verdict), which is
  what the depth requirement is for. Left as is; if a documented passage surfaces for lesson 9's
  work it could be back-ported.
