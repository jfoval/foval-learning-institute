   goes to "how long people actually work before switching", which is the exact phrase lesson 1 says
   this course does not repeat. **Fix it at publishing**, when the outcomes are re-read against the
   lessons that exist.

   abstract, which is the paper this course's lesson 2 is built on. Both lessons now say so. **The
   general point for the next course: check the earlier lesson's full text rather than its
   objectives**, because `npm run taught` prints objectives and an objective does not list every
   study a lesson uses.
3. **The standards 3.4 call was made at Stage 2 and re-read at Stage 4.** Psychology is on that
   list; the outline's judgement is that the list does not reach this subject, and the reviewer

   in the last five years", when one of the three is 2016). Both are fixed. **A research file's own
   framing sentences are worth checking as carefully as its quotations**, because a drafter will
   copy them.
