# Reviews: Focus and Deep Work lesson 6

## 2026-09-19 — Lesson 06 — Stage 4, all passes, Tier A
**Verdicts as returned:** **depth FAIL**, **fact-check FAIL**, neutrality pass with one fix, **pedagogy
FAIL**, cold start pass with fixes, voice and media pass with fixes.

### Findings

1. **[critical] Defect 22's second form, and the reviewer proved it by downloading the abstract.**
   The lesson stated twice, correctly, that it had read the abstract and not the body, and then gave
   a near-transfer figure of about g = 0.8 in three places, including a Sources entry attributing it
   to "reported effect sizes". **The abstract contains no numerical effect sizes at all** and does
   not use the phrase near transfer. The figure could only have come from the body the lesson says
   it did not open.
2. **[critical] The lesson did the one thing `SOURCES.md` told it not to, and then said it had
   not.** The research file says a course at this read level "must not put a figure in a learner's
   hands" for the mindfulness syntheses. The lesson printed four, and two paragraphs later wrote
   that it was not going to put a figure in your hands as though it had. **A reader who trusts the
   disclaimer carries the figures away believing they were withheld.**
3. **[major] The outline's first worked example was missing**, so the second exercise asked the
   reader to sort a training claim by transfer level with nothing worked first.
4. **[major] An unsourced characterisation of the other side's history**, that a large part of the
   earlier optimism came from comparisons against nothing.
5. **[major] Two of the six labels in the constructive section were wrong, in the same direction.**
   Elements 1 and 2 were labelled evidence where each is evidence plus a step this course takes from
   it: the popcorn experiments show an existing behaviour running off its context, not that choosing
   a context institutes a new hour; and lesson 2 establishes that a cost is hidden, not that
   removing interruptions improves an hour. **The judgement labels were all correct**, which is what
   the brief asked about, so the failure was the mirror of the one expected.
6. **[major] One Go deeper entry. Pedagogy 4.3: at most one of six items applied outside the
   lesson.**
7. **[medium]** Two of the course's own judgements stated as fact without markers. The meditation
   comparison assumed a scale the reader has never been given. Bold at 33.5 per 1,000, the highest
   of the three. The lesson never pointed at the project whose first section is its own six
   elements.

### Clean on inspection
**All five quotations exact**, against `SOURCES.md` and against the PMC abstract the reviewer
downloaded: the 87/145 count, the intermediate-and-far-transfer sentence, the mediation sentence,
the "no evidential value" sentence and the conclusion. The settled-against-contested call is right
and the lesson does not manufacture balance for the settled half. The "isn't this defeatist"
checkpoint engages the strongest objection to the whole course at full strength.

### Applied
All of them. The g = 0.8 figure is gone from all three places and replaced by the abstract's own
statement that the programmes produce specific training effects that do not generalise. The four
mindfulness figures are gone from the body and from the Sources entry, which now says why. A worked
example takes an invented training claim apart by transfer level before the exercise asks the reader
to. Elements 1 and 2 are relabelled "evidence, plus a step", with the step stated, and the summary
count follows. Two judgements marked, two quiz items recast as applications, a second Go deeper
entry, a forward pointer to the project, and the bold count cut.
