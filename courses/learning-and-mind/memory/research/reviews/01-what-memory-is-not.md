**And the reviewer recorded one thing worth carrying:** the route that produced four of the findings
was not an inflated read level but **unsourced material with no read level at all**, which is the

### Applied
All of them. The date is replaced with what the research file actually holds. The ninety-seven
percent figure is out of lesson 1 entirely, replaced by a qualitative statement carrying "initial",
"adults" and the five conditions, with the figures left to lesson 5. The three contrasts and the
four readings are labelled inline with their marker. Vividness is replaced by confidence throughout,
including the quiz key, because confidence is what this course has read something about. The
nonsense syllables, the real popular figure, and the weaker causal claim are all restored from
`SOURCES.md`. Two quiz items recast onto cases outside the lesson and the broken explanation fixed.
Bold cut from 28.7 to 19.6. And the boundary paragraph now says what it should have said: that the
earlier course used reconstruction to explain what builds a memory, and this one is about what
reconstruction does to one afterwards.
