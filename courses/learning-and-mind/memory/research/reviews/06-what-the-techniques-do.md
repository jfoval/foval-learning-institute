outline's demonstration missing), **fact-check FAIL**, neutrality pass with one reservation,

### Findings

1. **[critical] "Against an active control" is not in the abstract, and it carried the lesson's
   headline finding.** The abstract says only that memory training enhanced durable, longer-lasting
   memories; it does not name the comparison. The lesson asserted the memory arm beat the
   working-memory arm, which is exactly the result the three-arm design would be needed to show and
   exactly what the abstract declines to report. It appeared twice, in the honest-statement
   paragraph and in the checkpoint.
2. **[critical] Sources entry 2 claimed an inline marking that did not exist**, for the argument
   that the method of loci makes a weaker claim than brain training does. Third lesson in this
   course to ship the footnote-first pattern.
3. **[critical] Sources entry 3 made a provenance claim the read level cannot support**, saying the
   description of the technique and the strangeness instruction come from the source. The abstract
   names the technique in one clause and describes it not at all. Footnote 3 also had no marker
   anywhere in the body, so both were unreachable.
4. **[critical] Defect 22's third form on the memory-athletes claim.** "The people who memorise
   decks of cards and long strings of digits in competition are, almost without exception, using
   some version of it." Nothing in `SOURCES.md` says anything about what competitors do, and a quiz
   item graded the territory.
5. **[major] The trained material was asserted** as "the kind of list the technique is built for".
   The abstract names no task.
6. **[major] "The best-evidenced method available"**, a superlative over a literature the course
   read one paper of.
7. **[major] A false claim about Focus and Deep Work lesson 6**, that it found its adjacency move
   in a training advert. That lesson says in terms that its claim is invented.
8. **[major] An unsupported causal link between two of the course's own sources**, that lesson 3's
   effect is why a technique like this helps.
9. **[major] An unsourced claim about competitive memory performance**, that a competition
   memoriser would not be impressed by forty items.

11. **[major] No link anywhere in the teaching prose.**

    technique demonstrated on a short list, so the reader has done it before meeting the transfer

13. **[medium] "About fifty people in total" contradicted lesson 1's scope row**, which says about
    fifty plus the athletes.

### Clean on inspection
**No fabricated quotation.** The reviewer string-matched all eight quoted passages by hand; the
Wagner abstract is identical character for character including the diaeresis in "naïve", the
American spelling and the ungrammatical "a six-weeks memory training", which is what a re-typed
quotation loses. The read level is exact and the body discharges it twice, as its own Sources entry
claims. No number of words recalled appears anywhere in the lesson, which is what the whole lesson
is built to refuse. Contractions at 7.5 per 1,000, the first lesson in this repo that needed no
contraction pass. Quiz shape clean on all four measures. Decision 6 is not breached. Exercise 1 was
recorded as the best thing in the lesson, because it tells the reader what they will not learn from
doing it.

### Applied
All of them. The active-control over-read is gone from both places and the research file now records
in terms that the abstract does not name the comparison, or the material. The technique description
and the strangeness instruction carry markers and inline labels saying they are the technique as
generally described. The memory-athletes sentence is replaced by what the abstract does support,
which is that the athletes compared were ranked among the world's top 50. The Focus advert claim,
the superlative, the lesson 3 causal link and the competition-memoriser clause are all corrected or
cut. A worked demonstration is added before the transfer question, five items on a five-point route,
with a `:::predict` carrying the gap, which answers findings 12 and 20 together. Quiz items 3, 4 and
5 are recast onto a documentary, a nutrition trial and a university department. Bold cut to 19.6,
`minutes` to 90, the preprint linked in the prose, and the research file now carries the first
author's name and the URL.
