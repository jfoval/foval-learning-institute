**Verdicts as returned:** depth pass, **fact-check FAIL on the fabricated quotation alone**,
neutrality pass with reservations, **pedagogy fail**, cold start pass, **voice and media fail**.

### Findings

1. **[critical] A quotation attributed to the authors that appears nowhere in `SOURCES.md`.** The
   body printed, in quotation marks with a marker, the passage about exonerating the innocent and
   convicting the guilty, and then asserted "Both uses of 'initial' are theirs". The research file
   has no such passage; what it has is its own unquoted commentary making the same point. Every
   other quotation in the lesson string-matches the file exactly. This one sat in the section titled
   "The word the whole thing rests on" and was that section's sole evidence.
2. **[critical] Defect 22's second form in Sources entry 2.** It claimed the five explanations were
   consistent with the paper's sections on the first and fourth conditions, "which this course
   read". The read level is the Summary, Box 3, the section introducing the five recommendations,
   and one field-study passage. There are no per-condition sections in it.
3. **[critical] Defect 22's third form on the five explanations.** No marker, no inline label, and
   footnote 2 unreachable, while the entry said they were marked inline. The five sentences read
   exactly like the quoted material around them.
4. **[major] Quiz item 3's explanation mis-numbered Box 3**, counting condition two twice and
   omitting condition three.
5. **[major] "Initial" was missing where the strongest claim appears**, in the predict block's field
   figures and in quiz 5's stem and explanation. Outline decision 5 says the word appears wherever
   the claim does, and this is the lesson the decision was written for.
6. **[major] The traditional position's location was asserted without a source**, in textbooks and
   judicial guidance, which the research file does not support.
7. **[medium] "Four of which are precisely what the reform campaigns were for."** Nothing read says
   which of the five the campaigns sought.
8. **[medium] "Under procedures that jurisdiction had adopted."** An inference about the field
   study.
9. **[medium] An institute-wide superlative in Go deeper.**
10. **[low] Two negative claims about the paper at a four-section read level.**
11. **[major] Three of six items were recall of the page.**
12. **[low] Box 3 printed without its terminal full stops**, under the words "verbatim and
    complete".

### Clean on inspection
Every other quotation verified character for character: the Summary's opening, its question with the
parenthetical list, its two-part finding, its non-pristine qualification, Box 3, the low- and
high-confidence implication sentence, the 97 and 50 percent estimates, and the jurisdictions
qualification. The 72 and the 96 percent verified. The neutrality pass was run against the
traditional position, the reform-campaign position and a prosecution reading, and records that the
lesson refuses the comfortable reading in both directions and keeps the sentence most often dropped.
Exercise 2 is recorded as the best neutrality instrument in the four lessons.

### Applied
All of them. The fabricated quotation is gone and "initial" is grounded in the Summary sentence that
contains it and was verified. The five explanations are labelled at the head of the list as the
course's reasoning, footnote 2 has markers, and its entry now says none of them comes from the paper
and why. "Initial" is restored to the field figures and to quiz 5. Box 3's numbering is fixed in
quiz 3 and its full stops restored. The three unsupported claims are scoped or removed. Quiz items 2
and 5 are recast onto a forensic review with the same shape and a police force quoting its own
numbers. Bold cut from 28.5 to 18.7, and the synthesis is linked in the prose.

### Carried forward
**The fabricated quotation is the most serious single defect found in this repo so far**, because it
is the one class of error the institute's whole method is built to make impossible: a passage in
quotation marks, attributed, with a marker, that nobody wrote. It was caught by a reviewer
string-matching every quotation against `SOURCES.md`, which is now the thing to do first on any
lesson that quotes.
