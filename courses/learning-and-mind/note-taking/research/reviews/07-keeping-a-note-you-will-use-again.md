### Findings

1. **[critical] The closing recommendation said both its halves were "in the evidence", and the
   lesson says twice that one of them is not.** The lesson backed "a note that makes sense on its
   own, which is context, and going back to notes on purpose, which is review. Both of those are in
   the evidence." Sixty lines above, the predict block says "Nothing in its sources measures
   findability, context, or what makes a note usable a year later", and quiz item 2's key says the
   same. This is the one place in the lesson where the reader is told what to do, and it is the
   place SOURCES §7 told the drafter to make real and narrow. **Applied:** the two halves split, so
   review is named as the one part with a finding behind it and context as the course's own
   reasoning. The recommendation stays narrow and becomes true.
2. **[major] System B's yearly cost dropped a cost the lesson itself lists three lines above.**
   B is described as two minutes a day *plus a weekly fifteen minutes of review*, and its total was
   printed as twelve hours, which is the daily minutes alone. Redone by hand: A is 12 × 365 = 4,380
   minutes = 73.0 hours, correct; B is 730 + 780 = 1,510 minutes = about 25 hours. The section's
   ratio survives at three to one; the printed figure did not, and quiz item 3 was built on the
   twelve-against-two contrast. **Applied:** B stated at twenty-five with both parts shown, the
   point that A has no review step at all made explicit, the quiz item re-costed off different
   figures, and a two-bar chart added so the totals are visible at a glance.
3. **[major] Defect 22's third form, in the sentence carrying the whole lesson's warrant.**
   "Almost nobody has a routine at all" is an empirical claim about the population, in the course's
   voice, with no marker, source or label, and it **gained an intensifier** on its way out of
   `OUTLINE.md`. This is lesson 2 finding 3 repeating, including the intensifier. **Applied:** the
   project's own defensible sentence used instead, that almost nobody has ever measured whether
   they review anything, with the project named as what tests it.
4. **[major] Defect 22's second form: footnote 3 claimed a read level `SOURCES.md` does not
   record, and that level licensed a paragraph.** The ninety-thousand figure is recorded at
   search-summary level and is clean; the description of what a Zettelkasten is and what is claimed
   for it is in no source file at all, and the body printed a paragraph of it. **Applied:** the body
   now says, where the description begins, that none of the books was opened and that what follows
   is general knowledge rather than a reading; footnote 3 narrowed to the figure.
5. **[major] Outline decision 2 breached at the lesson's foundation.** "College", "lecture" and
   "student" appear in the body only inside the opening quotation, and everything built on it is a
   meeting, a report, a billing system and a notebook kept for a year. Lesson 1 rules out this exact
   move by name. Third recurrence in the course. **Applied:** a paragraph after the quotation
