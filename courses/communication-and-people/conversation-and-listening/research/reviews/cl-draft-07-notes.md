## A defect in SOURCES and the outline, corrected
- **Gilovich et al.: the observers were at chance, not "somewhat better than chance".** Part C §7.7 ("May not say: that people are 'unreadable'; observers did somewhat better than chance on disgust") and OUTLINE lesson 7 ("observers were somewhat better than chance on disgust, so 'unreadable' is wrong too") both say this. The paper says the opposite: in Study 2a the observers' 3.56 against a chance rate of 3.33 was not significant ("t < 1"), and the authors' summary of 2a and 2b is "Observers performed no better than chance". The lesson follows the paper. The caution that survives is a different one, and the lesson uses it: every study tested concealment, so they don't show that feelings people aren't hiding are unreadable, and the authors themselves say people are often well calibrated. Recorded as a Stage 1 correction in the gate-closure section. Please check Study 2a's results paragraph and the "Discussion of Studies 2a and 2b".
