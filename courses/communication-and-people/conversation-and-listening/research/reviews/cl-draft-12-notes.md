## Two corrections to SOURCES and OUTLINE the lesson depends on
1. **Mehl et al. 2010's −.33 is not comparable with Milek's .05.** Table 1: the −.33 is small talk as a
   percentage of conversations against a well-being index (life satisfaction plus happiness). Small talk
   as a share of all recordings was −.07 (index) and −.03 (life satisfaction) even in 2010. Milek's .05
   is the share-of-recordings measure; the like-for-like pair is −.33/−.25 against Milek's normalised
   −.08. OUTLINE's worked example (a) ("Mehl's r = −.33 against Milek's .05; substantive .28 against
   .23") and SOURCES Part B §7.4 pair different measures. The lesson and chart use like-for-like
   numbers from the life-satisfaction column and say why. **For the orchestrator:** OUTLINE lesson 12
   worked example (a) and Part B §7.4's "what a lesson may say" could carry this; not edited here.
2. **The Liu replication is not a replication of the Epley network's work.** Liu, Rim, Min and Min are a
   different lab; Study 4a is imagined outreach to a weak-tie acquaintance. The lesson says this course
   found no outside direct replication of the commuter or deep-talk experiments, and describes the
   Transparent Replications result as a related finding from a different lab (d .32 to .25).
