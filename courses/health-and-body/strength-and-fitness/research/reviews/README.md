# Stage 4 review records: Strength and Fitness

One file per lesson, named for the lesson, plus `course-wide.md` for findings that belong to the
course rather than to any one lesson. Each file records the review as it came back, what was applied,
and what was not applied and why.

**This course is a sensitive domain under standards 3.4** (health and medicine), so the neutrality
pass is mandatory on every lesson and the neutrality audit is mandatory before publish. The specific
risk here is not political. It is a training course sliding from teaching people to train inside
named general-population guidance (OUTLINE decision 3) into telling a reader what to do about a body
it has never examined, and a safety instruction a competent instructor would give going missing.

Lessons are reviewed at Tier B, split: Reviewer F takes facts, neutrality and safety, and Reviewer P
takes depth, pedagogy, cold start, and voice and media. A third context applies both sets of findings
and runs the voice checks, and it writes the entry.
