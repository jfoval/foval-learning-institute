### Findings

- **N1 (lessons 4 and 9; `SOURCES.md` 7.1).** Virta called "the low-carbohydrate side's best case"
  (L4), "The proponents' best case" (L9 heading) and "Lesson 4 promised the low-carbohydrate side's
  best case" (L9). The label is the research file's; the proponents' randomised evidence
  (Goldenberg, 57% against 31% remission at six months) is at least as strong. Failed Turing test.
- **N2 (lesson 4, WHO fact sheet).** "The first of those is an empirical claim" said of "a chronic,
  relapsing disease arising from ...". What it arises from is empirical; calling obesity itself a
  disease is a classification, and Rubino 2025 (in `SOURCES.md`, 9c) separates clinical from
  preclinical obesity. Misclassification (3.1).
- **N3 (lesson 5, misconception "The RDA is the amount you need").** Corrected only in the
  lower-need direction; the IAAO dispute in the body wasn't carried into the list, so a reader of
  the list alone takes the DRI estimate of need as settled.
- **N4 (lesson 10, VITAL).** Fish oil's secondary heart-attack result reported, vitamin D's
  secondary cancer-death result (0.83, 0.67 to 1.02, in the file) not. Selection (defect 14).
- **N5 (lesson 10, Bjelakovic).** Kidney-stone harm quoted, cancer-mortality reduction from the same
  abstract omitted. Selection.
- **N6 (lesson 10, ICCPUD).** "Yet its introduction leans on them": argumentative framing in the
  course's voice (3.3).
- **N7 (lesson 10, Who paid, MACH).** Donors left unnamed although the file recorded they were
  largely alcohol companies (search level). Omitted fact a critic of the J-curve would insist on.
- **N8 (lesson 7, Who paid, sugar).** ILSI named for Erickson, CIHR named for Sievenpiper, with
  "didn't read the disclosure statements" said only of the debate and Lustig, so the critics' trial
  evidence reads as cleanly funded. `SOURCES.md` notes Sievenpiper's industry disclosures from
  background knowledge only, unverified.
