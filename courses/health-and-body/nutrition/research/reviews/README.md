# Stage 4 review records: Nutrition: The Evidence

One file per lesson, plus `course-wide.md`. **A sensitive domain under standards 3.4** (nutrition and
medicine): every lesson is reviewed by two fresh-context reviewers in parallel, one for facts,
neutrality and safety and one for depth, pedagogy, cold start and voice, as Mental Fitness was, and
the neutrality audit is mandatory before publish. Two rather than five: the split keeps the safety
and neutrality pass separate, which is the one that matters most here, without paying five times for
passes that share their reading.

Each lesson is drafted in one fresh context, reviewed in two others, and fixed in a fourth, so no
context that wrote a sentence is the one that judges it.
