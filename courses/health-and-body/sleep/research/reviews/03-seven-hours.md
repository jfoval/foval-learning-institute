### Course-wide, from this review

- **The research file's own Objectives quotation is the best material available for finding 4 and was
  unused**: the authors frame their work as evidence about a relationship rather than a cause, in
  their own words. Defect 1. Worth using when lesson 8 is drafted.
- **"Base rate" against "baseline rate".** Logic and Argument teaches the concept as "base rate";
  this course says "baseline rate" throughout without noting they are the same. Worth one clause, and
  worth checking in lessons 5 to 8.
