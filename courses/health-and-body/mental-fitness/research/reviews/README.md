# Stage 4 review records: Mental Fitness

One file per lesson, plus `course-wide.md`. **A sensitive domain under standards 3.4** (mental health):
every lesson is reviewed by two fresh-context reviewers in parallel, one for facts, neutrality and
safety and one for depth, pedagogy, cold start and voice, and the neutrality audit is mandatory
before publish. Two rather than five: the split keeps the safety pass separate, which is the one
that matters most here, without paying five times for passes that share their reading.
