## 2026-09-26 — Lesson 03 fix pass: notes for later lessons

- **The US DEF position moved in July 2026.** EPA published a proposal on 14 July 2026 to replace
  derates on newly made diesel highway vehicles, light-duty included, with "audible and/or visible
  notifications"; the comment period closed 29 August 2026 and no final rule is listed. The 2022 rule
  is heavy-duty from model year 2027 on EPA's DEF page. Never use the 2025 fact sheet's "all new
  on-road diesel vehicles must..." as the current position. SOURCES "Stage 4 note, lesson 03". Lessons
  11 and 22 re-read the proposed-rule page before stating anything about derates.
- **The AA's wrong-tank advice is split by heading.** AdBlue in the fuel tank: "don't touch the
  ignition" and have it drained. Diesel in the AdBlue tank: "Don't turn on the ignition or start the
  engine and call us for help." SOURCES Part A §5 and OUTLINE lesson 3 are corrected. The podcast
  script and the course test's "AdBlue in the fuel tank" item use the first.
- **"Prime cause" is the RAC's sentence alone, and the RAC gives no buying advice in its own voice.**
  Only the AA advises against a DPF diesel for town use; the RAC's line about car makers is its claim,
  unchecked with any maker.
- **For the orchestrator (R26):** lesson 03 labels topping up AdBlue "Yours, on conditions" on the AA's
  "You can refill the Adblue tank yourself", with its conditions; decision 7's owner-by-hand list names
  only screenwash, oil and coolant. Confirm, and add a dated convention so lessons 9 and 11 label it
  the same way.
- **For lesson 04's fixer (R27):** lesson 04's key 0,2,3,1,0 shares a shifted run of four (lesson 03
  shifted by +3 is 0,2,3,1,3,2) at offset 0. Not a breach of the relaxed rule's letter; check it.
- **Exercise timing:** `npm run minutes` adds a stated "Take N minutes" on top of 2.5 per step, so N
  is the whole task's reading-and-looking time, not a top-up. Lesson 03's 25 cost 40 modelled minutes.
- **Quiz keys:** 01 3,0,1,3,2,0; 02 3,1,0,2,1; 03 1,3,0,2,0,3 (unchanged by the fix); 04 0,2,3,1,0.

## 2026-09-26: lesson 02 Stage 4 (fixer's notes)

- **Quiz keys:** 02 is 3,1,0,2,1 after the fix (unchanged); rechecked by script against 01, 03 and 04
  on disk under the relaxed rule, value shifts included. Passes.
- **Oil's job is sourced now; its mechanism is not.** The RAC warning-lights page: "If the oil is not
  lubricating the engine effectively it could lead to expensive or even irreparable engine damage,
  so it's important to act quickly." [V] (SOURCES Part B §3). Lessons 9, 11 and 12 may say oil
  lubricates, on the RAC's word; G-H12 stays open, so nothing about how.
- **The RAC's cambelt signs and its no-symptoms line travel together.** Between the five signs and
  "Many cambelts show no obvious symptoms before failure." the RAC says to have a car showing any sign
  "checked as soon as possible by a qualified mechanic". Any later lesson (9, 21, 24) that quotes one
  quotes the other. SOURCES "Gate closures, lesson 02" is corrected.
- **AFDC's word is "combustion chamber".** Don't gloss it as "cylinder" or call it "direct injection"
  in the course's voice; no source read uses that term.
- **fueleconomy.gov's energy diagrams have five rows**, including "Auxiliary Electrical Losses: 0% -
  2%"; the ranges can't be added to one total. Lesson 4 or 12, if it prints them, uses all five.
- **DENSO says nothing about cold weather**; its light comes on at every start. A cold-morning framing
  is the course's reading and is labelled as such.
