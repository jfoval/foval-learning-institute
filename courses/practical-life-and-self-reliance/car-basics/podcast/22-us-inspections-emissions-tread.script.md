S2: Now the big one. Does periodic safety inspection reduce crashes?

S1: It's a contested empirical question, with a value question behind it, how much cost and inconvenience a possible safety gain is worth, which is for each state's voters and legislators. S1: Careful. GAO found no studies establishing that inspections reduce crashes, and in its words, this does not necessarily demonstrate that inspections do not have such an effect.
