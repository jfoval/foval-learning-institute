---

This test covers the whole course, and most of its questions are about passages and cases the
lessons did not work for you. That is deliberate. The point of the twelve lessons was not that you
would remember what they said about Mark 16 or Isaiah 7:14, but that you could do the same thing
yourself with a chapter nobody has explained to you.

You need 80 percent to pass, and you can retake it. If you miss questions from one lesson, reread
that lesson rather than the test.

One thing the test cannot measure: whether you can run the method on a book from a cold start, with
nobody's answer to check against. That is what the project is for.
