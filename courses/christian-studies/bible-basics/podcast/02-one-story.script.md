S1: The course borrows a map from a book called The Drama of Scripture. Six acts. Creation. Fall. Israel. Jesus. Church. New creation. And before you lean on it, two honest things. First, it's a teaching device. Scripture doesn't number its own acts, and other good teachers cut the story in other places. There's an eight stage version, and the Catholic catechism lays the same story out by covenants instead of acts. None of those teachers disagrees about what happened. They disagree about where the natural breaks fall, and none of the breaks is inspired.
