S1: Three parts. And not three signals to look for in the writing. Three parts of the situation. One, contact you did not initiate. Something arrived, you weren't in the middle of doing this.

S1: Pressure that makes thinking expensive. A deadline, a consequence. An account about to close, a parcel about to go back.

S1: And puts a cheap action right next to it. One click and it's handled. The pressure doesn't make you stupid. It changes the price of thinking, and then offers you a discount.

S2: So what's the move?
