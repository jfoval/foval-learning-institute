Arguments: `$ARGUMENTS` (school slug, course id, title in quotes). Verify the school slug is one of the 15 in `curriculum/TAXONOMY.md` and the course appears in that school's table. If it does not, add the row yourself (root `CLAUDE.md` rule 8: placing a course on the map is an ordinary judgement call), unless the course would change what the institute claims or teaches, in which case bring it to John. Before anything else, settle the course's **Path** cell: read "Placing a course on the Core" in TAXONOMY.md and place the course in a term (`T1` to `T25`), which also means adding it to that term in `curriculum/core-path.yaml` in the position where it should be taken. There is no elective tier. Say which term you chose and why. `npm run validate` fails while the cell is blank, so this is not optional and cannot be left for later. Create `courses/<school>/<course-id>/` with `research/`, `lessons/`, and `assessments/` folders, copy `templates/course.yaml` in and fill it: id, title, school, subject (the school's display name), level from the taxonomy row, `status: research`, and a first-pass summary, description, outcomes, and prerequisites based on the taxonomy note. Update the TAXONOMY.md row to `research`. Run `npm run validate` before you finish. Then say where the course landed on the Core and that the next step is `/research-course courses/<school>/<course-id>`.
