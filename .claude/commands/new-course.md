---
description: Stage 0. Scaffold a new course folder from templates
argument-hint: <school-slug> <course-id> "<Course Title>"
---
Arguments: `$ARGUMENTS` (school slug, course id, title in quotes). Verify the school slug is one of the 15 in `curriculum/TAXONOMY.md` and the course appears in that school's table (if not, ask the owner whether to add it). Before anything else, settle the course's **Path** cell: read "Placing a course on the Core" in TAXONOMY.md and either place the course in a term (`T1` to `T8`, which also means adding it to that term in `curriculum/core-path.yaml` in the position where it should be taken) or mark it `elective`. Say which you chose and why. `npm run validate` fails while the cell is blank, so this is not optional and cannot be left for later. Create `courses/<school>/<course-id>/` with `research/`, `lessons/`, and `assessments/` folders, copy `templates/course.yaml` in and fill it: id, title, school, subject (the school's display name), level from the taxonomy row, `status: research`, and a first-pass summary, description, outcomes, and prerequisites based on the taxonomy note. Update the TAXONOMY.md row to `research`. Run `npm run validate` before you finish. Then tell the owner where the course landed on the Core and that the next step is `/research-course courses/<school>/<course-id>`.
